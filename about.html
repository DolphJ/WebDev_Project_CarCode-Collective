About Us

I’m John O’Reilly, the proud owner of Wheels on Fire and, depending on who you ask, a man with a slightly unhealthy obsession with cars. I’ve been in the car business for as long as I can remember. I’ve always believed that a great car should be more than just a way of getting from A to B. It should have character. It should put a smile on your face when you turn the key. And, ideally, it shouldn’t make any alarming noises halfway down the road. I’m a bit of a perfectionist. I like things done properly, from a perfectly tuned engine to a car that looks just right sitting on the forecourt. I’ll happily spend hours listening to an engine, checking the details, and making sure everything is as it should be. I’m probably more particular about cars than I am about people although cars tend to complain less. Over the years, I’ve built up a collection of cars that I’m genuinely proud of, each with its own character, history, and quirks. Some are beautiful, some are fast, some are wonderfully impractical, and a few are all three. That’s what makes cars interesting. Wheels on Fire is about sharing that passion. Whether you’re looking for your next dream car, something reliable with a bit of personality, or simply want to admire some seriously good machinery, you’re in the right place. I’m here to help you find a car you’ll love because life’s too short to drive something boring. And if you happen to hear an engine running perfectly when you visit, there’s a very good chance I’ve been tinkering with it..
